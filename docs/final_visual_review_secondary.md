# Secondary final visual review

Reviewed on 12 October 2026 from the actual final PNGs: hero, front, left,
right, back, top, head, eye, mouth, crown, body_trim, brocade, paw and tail,
plus all five final clay views. Compared with `references/reference_original.jpg`.
Draft and revision images were excluded from this review. No model was edited.

The full views clearly read as a Guangfu lion: the raised red brows, white eye
frames, four lobed paws, long beard, waved body trim and raised white tail are
legible. Dense scarlet fibers and ivory strands improve the material distinction.
The fitted glossy eyes sit visibly within the frames; there is no obvious
detached eye globe in the whole or head views. Reflective caps/bells and separate
white teeth provide clearer localized structure. The new body and central
forehead brocade has substantially sharper motifs and more continuous coverage
than the blurred source patterns surrounding it.

Important remaining art limitations, in priority order:

1. **Crown:** `crown.png` shows faceted, fused rings and a forward projecting
   knob with soft baked gold/red detail. Its shape and material response differ
   from the reference's clean concentric metal rings and furry red ornament.
   Jagged source surfaces remain on the adjacent ear/brow tops.
2. **Nose and mouth:** `head.png` and `mouth.png` show faceted tricolor nose
   balls, coarse white nose curls, blurred black/gold markings and broad pink
   cheek/lip sheets. The white tooth row is clear but fairly straight; the
   reference has a more curved mouth rim and a decorated red interior. Angular
   original white beard strips remain visible behind the added strands.
3. **Source-surface boundaries:** `brocade.png`, `body_trim.png` and `top.png`
   retain isolated red triangular fins/islands on the cloth and abrupt cloth/
   trim junctions. The clay views expose irregular pits/notches and ribbon-like
   beard, neck and tail surfaces. Fiber coverage softens these features without
   fully repairing their underlying geometry.
4. **Pattern fidelity:** the crisp brocade is a documented synthesized design.
   Its regular flowers/repeats differ from the reference's denser gold/black
   pattern. New-versus-old forehead boundaries remain visible. Rear head,
   cheeks, crown and parts of the limbs still retain pale, softened source
   motifs; close views do not establish individually modeled embroidery.
5. **Groom and accessories:** beard/tail strands give volume, but some original
   long strips show through. Tassels still expose flat cone/blade shapes rather
   than uniformly hanging fine threads. `paw.png` has exposed smooth white toe
   areas, patchy strand density and a blurred gold bead-band texture. Several
   limb fur bases remain much pinker than the scarlet torso trim.

The final images support an editable, reference-inspired asset with substantial
local refinement. Exact physical proportions, ornament construction, original
motif placement and finished close-up surface fidelity remain approximate.
